01 / FINITE-STATE MACHINE

The 9 Requisition States Visual Architecture

Deterministic transactional states governing requisition flow inside PRSYS. Transitions are logged to the immutable audit ledger with timestamps, actor IDs, and hash integrity.

02 PENDING Sign-off

01 • DRAFT Pre-Commitment

Initial requester drafting stage. Cart items, catalog references, and vendor quotes are uncommitted. No SLA timer runs, and no budget reservation is locked against department cost centers.

Actor: Requester Only
Next Transition: PENDING_APPROVAL
02 • PENDING_APPROVAL SLA Active (24-48h)

Queued inside the multi-tier approval matrix awaiting electronic signatures. Cost center funds are provisionally frozen. Approvers receive automated escalation triggers upon SLA expiry.

Actor: Authorized Approver(s)
Next Transition: APPROVED | REJECTED | CLARIFY
03 • CLARIFICATION_REQUESTED SLA Paused

Temporarily frozen pending requester response. Triggered when technical specifications, vendor quotes, or tax documentation (e.g. 14% VAT certificate) are missing or non-compliant.

Actor: Requester or Reviewer
Next Transition: PENDING_APPROVAL | CANCELLED
04 • APPROVED Budget Locked

All necessary departmental, operational, and executive authorizations successfully recorded. The requisition is handed over to Sourcing/Procurement agents to generate formal vendor purchase orders.

Actor: Procurement Officer
Next Transition: PO_REGISTERED
05 • PO_REGISTERED ERP Synced (SAP)

Formal Purchase Order dispatched to external supplier. Requisition record stamped with external PO # and linked to ERP ledger. Legal procurement obligation established with supplier.

Actor: ERP System / Buyer
Next Transition: FULFILLED
06 • FULFILLED GRN Stamped

Vendor items delivered to warehouse or services rendered on-site. Verified via Goods Received Note (GRN) or Technical Acceptance Sheet signed by inventory dispatcher or project owner.

Actor: Warehouse / Requester
Next Transition: COMPLETED
07 • COMPLETED Final Terminal

Three-way matching (PO vs GRN vs Vendor Tax Invoice) validated by Accounts Payable. Final disbursement released to vendor. Ticket closed and transferred to long-term compliance archive.

Actor: Finance & AP System
Next Transition: NONE (LOCKED)
08 • REJECTED Mandatory Remarks

Turned down by an approver or compliance reviewer. Requires mandatory justification remarks submitted into the audit log. Reserved cost center funds are released back to departmental budget immediately.

Actor: Designated Approver
Next Transition: RE-DRAFT (NEW REQ ID)
09 • CANCELLED Pre-PO Termination

Voided voluntarily by requester or super-administrator prior to PO commitment. Allows cancellation if commercial terms dissolved or requirements changed before contractual execution.

Actor: Requester or Super Admin
Next Transition: NONE (TERMINAL)
